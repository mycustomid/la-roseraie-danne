01 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before your visit, here is what to clarify.

Some details still need confirmation by the venue. We prefer a clear answer to an unsupported promise.

Contact↗
01Where is La Roseraie d’Anne located?+

The venue is described as being in Mvan, in the Yaoundé area of Cameroon. Ask for an exact route and meeting point before travelling.

02How can I arrange a private viewing?+

Use the “Arrange a visit” button or the contact form. A visit is confirmed only once the team replies.

03How many guests can the venue accommodate?+

Actual capacity depends on the setting, event configuration, equipment and safety requirements. No figure is published until it has been verified.

04Are prices and packages available?+

Use the enquiry form to describe your requirements. Rates, included services, tax and payment conditions should be specified in a written proposal.

05Can I host a traditional ceremony?+

You can submit traditional ceremonies for review. Discuss the rituals, timetable and logistical requirements directly with the team.

06Is catering or décor included?+

Services depend on the project and confirmed supplier availability. No partner service is assumed to be included without agreement.

07Are accommodation and suites available?+

Suites feature in the original concept brief. Their actual existence, facilities and availability require direct confirmation.

08Is a deposit required?+

Booking, deposit and cancellation terms should be supplied by the venue as part of its formal offer or contract.

09Can I plan an event from abroad?+

Mention your country and availability in your enquiry. You can request a remote video walkthrough, subject to confirmation.

10Are these photographs of the real estate?+

Not necessarily. The current photographs are editorial assets from the original repository and are not authenticated venue photos.
